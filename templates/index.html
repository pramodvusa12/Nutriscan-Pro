About the Project

Smart nutrition at a glance

NutriScan Pro uses deep learning to detect food type, freshness, and nutritional content in seconds — no lab required.

Built as a CSP project, NutriScan Pro combines computer vision and nutrition databases to give you real-time food quality insights. Whether you're at a grocery store or cleaning out your fridge, a single photo tells you everything you need to know.

The system detects multiple food categories including fruits, vegetables, and more. It classifies freshness with a confidence score and adjusts nutrition values accordingly.

What We Offer

Powerful Detection Capabilities

🍎

Multi-Category Detection

Identifies fruits, vegetables and more using an EfficientNetV2 backbone with transfer learning.

🧪

Freshness Analysis

CNN-based fresh/spoiled classifier trained on a curated dataset returns a confidence score with every result.

📊

Nutrition Breakdown

Instant calories, protein, and fat data from our curated database. Values adjust automatically for spoiled food.

📅

Preservation Guidance

Know exactly how many days a food item can be safely stored, with personalized storage tips.

📱

Any Image Source

Accepts photos from your mobile gallery, WhatsApp downloads, Google images, or directly from your camera.

🌐

Platform Independent

Works on any device — Android, iOS, Windows, Mac — through a responsive web interface. No app install needed.
